Do bigger homes sell for more?

Each dot is one eligible home sale. The horizontal axis shows finished living area; the vertical axis shows sale price.

To keep invalid and documented extreme values from distorting the view, this plot excludes records with missing or nonpositive living area, sale prices below $1,000, and sale prices above $5,000,000. The documentation flags zero square-foot living areas and prices above $5 million as implausible. The $1,000 lower price limit follows its median-price convention.

A median is the middle sale price after eligible sales are ordered; it is less affected by unusually high or low prices than an average. Sale prices are reported by the collection source and have not been independently verified against county records. Individual towns may include unusual prices above the $1,000 cutoff.

Source: College-town home sales dataset, version 1.0 (2026). University of Illinois-Deloitte Foundation Center for Business Emerging Technologies. Data collected March 2026.
